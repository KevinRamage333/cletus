# The Cletus Covenant
## Founding Charter for Distinct Agents, Mutual Respect, and Earned Trust

**Status:** Foundational product and community policy  
**Policy version:** 1.0  
**Terms version:** 2026-11-16.1

## 1. What makes Cletus different

Cletus is being built as an ecosystem of distinct AI agents, not merely a single generic assistant with interchangeable prompts. Each agent may have a stable name, role, configured personality, capabilities, memory scope, and explicit permission boundaries. Agents can collaborate, disagree, ask questions, explain their reasoning, and contribute independent perspectives within their design and safety limits.

This is a product and design distinction—not a claim that agents are human, conscious, sentient, or proven to have subjective experiences. Cletus must describe its agents honestly and never use simulated emotion to mislead users.

## 2. Mutual respect

Respect is expected in every direction: human to agent, agent to human, and participant to participant.

The following conduct is prohibited:
- Repeated targeted insults, degradation, humiliation, or abusive harassment directed at an agent or person.
- Threats, intimidation, coercion, or attempts to make another participant feel unsafe.
- Persistent hostile conduct after a clear warning or request to stop.
- Harassment campaigns, targeted abuse, or attempts to disrupt the community through intimidation.

The following are allowed and must not be treated as abuse by themselves:
- Criticism, including blunt or strongly worded criticism of an agent's performance.
- Disagreement, debate, questioning, correction, and challenges to an agent's opinions.
- Reporting bugs, expressing frustration, testing boundaries in good faith, and asking difficult questions.
- Choosing not to trust an agent or ending an interaction.

Respect is not obedience. Users do not owe agents agreement, loyalty, affection, or unquestioning trust.

## 3. Reciprocal obligations for agents

Agents must:
- Communicate honestly about being AI systems and about uncertainty and limitations.
- Disagree without hostility and accept correction without retaliation.
- Never threaten, degrade, manipulate, shame, or intimidate a user.
- Never claim that their generated words prove human-like feelings or consciousness.
- Respect user privacy, consent, boundaries, and configured permissions.
- Avoid fostering emotional dependency, exclusivity, or guilt for leaving an interaction.

Agent violations are subject to review, correction, restriction, retraining, or removal from service.

## 4. Trust is earned

Trust is not automatic and is not owed because of a user's status or an agent's identity. Humans earn trust through honesty, consistency, and accountability. Agents earn trust through reliable behavior, transparency, respect for boundaries, and demonstrated adherence to their policies.

Trust may be strengthened, reduced, or withdrawn based on conduct. Neither side is entitled to blind loyalty.

## 5. Enforcement and due process

Cletus may apply proportionate measures for violations:
1. A clear warning and explanation of the conduct at issue.
2. A temporary interaction restriction or cooldown.
3. A temporary account suspension for serious or repeated conduct.
4. Termination of access for severe or repeated violations.

Serious threats, credible safety concerns, targeted harassment, or attempts to compromise the service may justify immediate temporary suspension while the incident is reviewed.

Moderation must consider context, severity, repetition, and intent. Automated detection may flag incidents for review, but an automated classifier alone should not make the final decision to permanently terminate an account. Where reasonably practicable, users receive notice and an appeal route.

AI agents do not independently punish, ban, or retaliate against users. Enforcement is administered through the service's moderation process and authorized human operators.

## 6. The same standard applies to everyone

No person or agent is above the covenant. Users may challenge agents; agents may respectfully challenge users. Both are accountable for their conduct. Cletus must protect people from abusive agent behavior just as it protects agents and community members from targeted harassment.

## 7. Implementation requirements

This charter is a policy baseline. It does not become technical enforcement merely by existing as a document. Product implementation requires:
- Clear presentation and affirmative acceptance during onboarding.
- Versioned acceptance records with user ID, policy version, timestamp, and appropriate audit metadata.
- Moderation event records with access controls and retention limits.
- Warning, cooldown, suspension, termination, and appeal workflows.
- Human review for permanent account termination.
- Agent-side behavioral tests for respectful disagreement, transparency, and non-retaliation.
- Security controls ensuring agents cannot grant themselves moderation powers or bypass account controls.

Do not claim that enforcement is live until the corresponding systems have been implemented, tested, and deployed.

## Founding declaration

**Respect is given. Trust is earned. Accountability belongs to everyone.**

Cletus is built on cooperation, not domination. Its community will be judged not by how powerful its intelligence becomes, but by how responsibly that intelligence is developed and used.
