## 2026-11-11: Whitepaper and public roadmap

Content covers vision, how Cletus works (Brain, scanner, order-flow / falling-knife reading, Helius whale tracking, X hype, RugCheck, risk presets, stops and fast exit), non-custodial design, Jupiter execution, the 0.5% fee, transparency (per-user live view and the public P&L feed), security, team / Cletus Technologies LLC, and a phased roadmap in the owner's order: Solana (live now, in beta) → Base → Ethereum → Robinhood Chain and other networks (BSC, Arbitrum) → stocks and forex. | 2026-11-11 | Self-directed adaptive exits ("Cletus picks his own exits") for live owner trades | Instead of fixed stop-loss / take-profit constants, each live trade gets its own exit plan at entry (and recomputed once for an older open position): the stop distance (risk unit R) comes from the coin's own recent volatility (DexScreener m5/h1/h6/h24 price change scaled to %/hour), scaled by entry score and pool liquidity; the partial take, break-even trigger, trailing stop and final target are R-multiples adjusted by the market regime (breadth and median 1 h move of the scanned band); a bounded, gradual learning loop tunes the stop width and partial target from the MFE/MAE of the bot's own closed trades (no effect under 8 trades, weight capped at 0.5). Taper framework kept (partial sell, then break-even, trail, final exit). Hard bounds: SL 8–25%, partial +15–100% selling 33–60%, trail 5–15%, final ≤ +200%, daily loss stop unchanged. Plans are persisted per position and logged at entry / restart. Out-of-sample replay on 3.6 days of candles: adaptive+learning beat fixed 5/20 and matched or beat the fixed taper on every train/test split tried, on ~15–25 test trades each (small sample, not a proven edge). See `src/lib/adaptive-exits.ts`, `scripts/backtest/adaptive-exits.ts`. |

| 2026-11-11 | Volume-fade exit ("dead coin, free the slot") for live owner positions | If a held coin's 5-minute volume stays below 10% of its level at entry (or buyers fall under 20% of txns on under half the entry volume) for 20 continuous minutes, while the trade is below +15% and before its first partial take, Cletus sells to free the single position slot for the next coin.
