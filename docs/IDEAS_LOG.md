## 2026-11-11: Whitepaper and public roadmap
Public `/whitepaper` page (gold site style) plus a downloadable PDF at `/cletus-whitepaper.pdf`, linked from the site nav and footer. Content covers vision, how Cletus works (Brain, scanner, order-flow / falling-knife reading, Helius whale tracking, X hype, RugCheck, risk presets, stops and fast exit), non-custodial design, Jupiter execution, the 0.5% fee, transparency (per-user live view and the public P&L feed), security, team / Cletus Technologies LLC, and a phased roadmap in the owner's order: Solana (live now, in beta) → Base → Ethereum → Robinhood Chain and other networks (BSC, Arbitrum) → stocks and forex. Phases use milestone gates (for example, "after Solana is proven profitable") and no calendar dates. The document states that the strategy is experimental, that backtests have not shown an edge, that there is no token, and includes a full not-financial-advice / high-risk disclaimer. No profitability, returns, or regulatory-compliance claims.
